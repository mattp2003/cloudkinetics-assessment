# Shipping Policy

## Standard Shipping
- Delivery time: 5–7 business days
- Free on orders over $50; $5.99 flat fee for orders under $50
- Available for all US domestic addresses including PO Boxes

## Expedited Shipping
- Delivery time: 2–3 business days
- Flat fee: $12.99 regardless of order size
- Available for all contiguous US states

## Overnight Shipping
- Delivery time: next business day
- Flat fee: $24.99
- Orders must be placed before 2:00 PM PST to ship the same day
- Not available for Alaska, Hawaii, or PO Boxes

## International Shipping
- Available to select countries only (Canada, UK, Australia, Germany, France, Japan)
- Delivery time: 10–21 business days depending on destination and customs
- Fees calculated at checkout based on weight and destination
- Customer is responsible for any applicable customs duties or import taxes

## Order Tracking
- A tracking number is emailed to you when the shipping label is printed
- Tracking updates in real time via our website or the carrier's portal
- Allow up to 24 hours after label creation for the first scan to appear

## Lost or Missing Packages
- If your package has not arrived 10 business days beyond the estimated delivery date, contact our customer service team
- We will open an investigation with the carrier within 1 business day
- Replacements or refunds are issued within 5 business days of confirmed loss

## APO/FPO Military Addresses
- We proudly ship to APO and FPO addresses
- Delivery time: 14–30 business days
- Standard shipping rates apply; expedited and overnight options are not available for these addresses
