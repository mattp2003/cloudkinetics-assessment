# Return Policy

## Return Window
- Standard items: 30 days from the delivery date
- Electronics (laptops, tablets, phones, audio equipment): 15 days from delivery date
- Final sale items: clearly marked as "Final Sale" at checkout — not eligible for return or exchange

## Item Condition Requirements
- Items must be unused and in their original condition
- Original packaging, tags, and all included accessories must be present
- Items showing signs of use, damage, or missing components may be refused or subject to a restocking fee of up to 20%

## Return Shipping
- Defective or incorrect items: free return label provided by us, full refund issued
- All other returns: $5.99 prepaid return label fee deducted from your refund
- You may also use your own carrier; we recommend insuring the shipment as we are not responsible for lost returns

## How to Start a Return
1. Email support@ck-ecommerce.com with your order ID and reason for return
2. Our team will respond within 1 business day with a return authorization and label
3. Pack the item securely and drop it off at any authorized carrier location
4. Keep your tracking receipt until your refund is confirmed

## Refund Timeline
- Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after the warehouse receives and inspects your return
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method
- Credit card refunds may take an additional 3–5 business days to appear on your statement depending on your bank

## Exchanges
- We do not offer direct exchanges; please return the original item and place a new order
- If you received a defective item, contact us and we will ship a replacement at no charge before you return the original

## Non-Returnable Items
- Downloadable software or digital products
- Perishable goods
- Customized or personalized items
- Hazardous materials

## Questions
For return inquiries, contact support@ck-ecommerce.com or call 1-800-555-0100 (Mon–Fri, 9 AM–6 PM PST).
